                ollama run llama3.2

                Download Resume Lab AI Studio
                Download ZIP ⬇

3

            

              Double-Click the 1-Click Launch File!
              

                Inside the extracted folder, simply double-click:
              

              

                

                  🪟 Windows:
                  start_studio.bat
                

                

                  🍎 Mac / Linux:
                  ./start_studio.sh
                

              

              

                ✨ It automatically sets up Python, installs dependencies, and opens http://localhost:5050 in your browser!
